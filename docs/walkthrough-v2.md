# Three-minute portfolio walkthrough

## 0:00–0:30 — Establish the customer problem
“This is an independent fictional consulting example. The Head of Talent says engineering interviews feel slow. I would first agree which roles, stage definitions and business outcome matter, and hear the recruiter and hiring manager perspectives.”

Open two discovery questions. Explain how their answers change the investigation.

## 0:30–1:20 — Show evidence and challenge it
Open Funnel investigation. Engineering has an 11-day completed technical/functional-stage median in the bundled example; show the valid sample and timestamp coverage. Active waiting time is displayed separately.

Switch to Commercial: the example median is four days. Explain that this comparison is descriptive and needs role-mix and workflow context. Inspect October separately, noting different observation time. Do not attribute a cause to interviewer performance.

Missing dates reduce duration coverage. Explain how you would reconcile records in an authorised source system before recommending a change. The history is a synthetic educational extension, not reconstructed real customer data.

## 1:20–1:55 — Teach and enable
Open Teach the team. Explain the difference between completed duration and active waiting. Use the response exercise to demonstrate how you would handle a sponsor who jumps to a cause.

Quality of Hire and time-to-fill are unavailable from this evidence. Say what additional definitions and data would be required.

## 1:55–2:35 — Scope and align
Open Scope & handover. Explain deliverables, proposed acceptance criteria, customer responsibilities and exclusions. Change one objective. Describe how you would handle an out-of-scope integration request and when the CSM should involve a specialist.

## 2:35–3:00 — Deliver and establish boundaries
Open Customer report. Show that the objective and selected evidence carry through. Download the report and filtered metrics. Mention the existing record audit, repair queue and candidate-ID import safeguards below.

Conclude: “This demonstrates my consulting approach. I would validate the customer's definitions and any configuration change in their authorised Ashby instance. The example makes no claim of real customer outcomes or native product configuration.”
