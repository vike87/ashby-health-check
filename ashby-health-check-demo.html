Privacy, responsible use & legal limits

CSV contents stay in this browser tab. This app has no analytics, cookies, candidate database, external scripts or AI API calls. Hosting and sign-in providers may process access logs independently. Reload or use Clear data to remove application data from this app; downloaded files and clipboard copies remain on your device.

Use pseudonymous application IDs where possible. Names and emails are optional; email is needed only for duplicate checks. Exclude CVs, interview notes, special-category data and unnecessary identifiers. Exported repair queues can contain personal data: protect them and apply your organisation’s retention policy.

For authorised recruiting operations staff only. This tool checks records; it does not assess, rank or select candidates, infer personal characteristics, or make employment decisions. AI review is an optional manual workflow. Counts below five are suppressed and other counts bucketed; review the remaining context before sharing. No candidate data is automatically sent to Le Chat.

This prototype is not certified GDPR or EU AI Act compliant. Your organisation must establish lawful basis, transparency, access controls and retention, assess processors and transfers, and decide whether a DPIA is required. Any future AI functionality that evaluates candidates requires a fresh legal and risk assessment. Read the compliance notes included in the source package.
